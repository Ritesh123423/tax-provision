Step 3 of 8WP-3

Deferred tax schedule

Compare the carrying amount of every asset and liability with its tax base. Each line carries its own opening tax effect, so the schedule articulates on its own.

Rule
Temporary difference = carrying amount − tax base. Assets: a carrying amount above the tax base is a taxable difference giving a liability. Liabilities: a carrying amount above the tax base is a deductible difference giving an asset. Where a provision is deductible only on payment, its tax base is nil — enter 0 rather than leaving it blank.
